FONTLOG for Talyn Brush

Talyn Brush is an independent modified version of Nanum Brush Script.
It is distributed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1. See OFL.txt.

2026-10-01: Talyn Brush 0.400 (release tag v0.4.0; preview)
- Redrew every constructed stroke at the original pen weight after visual
  review of 0.300 found thin, straight flags and crossbars (г measured 27
  units against 55-70 in the source) and squeezed stems in Г, П, Ш and Щ.
- New rule in the recipes: a stroke is stretched only along its own length.
  Stems come from the original l and dotless i, bars from the hyphen and the
  middle bar of E, with no scaling across their thickness.
- Rebuilt Г from F without its middle bar, Л and Д from A without its
  crossbar, Ц from U, н and к on dotless i stems, ү as a v fork on a
  straight stem, л as a rotated v with a thin entry and heavy right leg.
- Replaced thin fitted dashes in Ө ө Э э Ю ю Б б Ъ ъ ₮ with full-weight
  brush bars; shortened the д descender to sit with р.
- Redrew ß as the original f without its crossbar joined to the native s,
  removing the knotted head of 0.300; ẞ follows.
- Added a stroke-weight regression test that rasterises every added letter
  and rejects strokes thinner than the source pen.
- Added polygon cuts to the recipe tool so a stroke can be separated from a
  neighbouring one along its own edge.
- Retained all inherited glyphs, metrics, hinting, GSUB and licensing data.

2026-10-01: Talyn Brush 0.300 (release tag v0.3.0; preview)
- Added German Ä Ö Ü ä ö ü ß ẞ, all missing from the original font.
- Composed umlauts from original Latin bodies and native brush dots, with
  unchanged base advances and optical mark positions shared with GPOS.
- Added lowercase/capital sharp S using original long-s and s movements.
- Added German proof images, a Deutsch tester sample, normalization,
  glyph-coverage, metric, accent-contour and small-size rendering checks.
- Complete cmap now contains 11,871 mappings: 11,789 original and 82 added.
- Retained the revised Cyrillic outlines from 0.200 and every upstream glyph.
- Updated derivative copyright wording to credit German as well as Cyrillic.

2026-10-01: Talyn Brush 0.200 (release tag v0.2.0; preview)
- Reworked the Cyrillic design after feedback on rigid miniature capitals.
- Introduced handwritten lowercase forms for и, п, т, д and ш, and reshaped
  the other constructed letters using actual original brush contours.
- Removed synthetic pressure ribbons; retained original stroke texture,
  irregular terminals, and full-weight bowls when shortening ascenders.
- Refined Өө / Үү, Фф, Жж, marks, crossbars and curved descenders.
- Unioned components before final outline conversion to prevent reversed
  contour winding from cutting gaps into overlapping brush strokes.
- Added counter/connected-contour regression tests and CI word-size proofs.
- Compared identical phrases against the first preview and original Latin.
- Retained all inherited glyphs and licensing metadata without changes.
- Remains a preview awaiting independent native Mongolian/type-design review.

2026-10-01: Talyn Brush 0.100 (release tag v0.1.0; preview)
- Preserved the exact user-supplied original TTF and OFL in upstream/.
- Preserved all 11,789 original Unicode mappings and every original glyph
  outline, advance width, sidebearing, hinting program, and GSUB rule.
- Added all 35 Mongolian Cyrillic letters in both cases (70 mappings),
  including barred O and straight U. Also covers the modern Russian alphabet.
- Added combining breve and diaeresis, nonbreaking space, and tugrik sign.
- Added mark attachment and selected Cyrillic kerning pairs.
- Renamed primary font names to Talyn Brush / TalynBrush-Regular, respecting
  all original Reserved Font Names. Preserved original copyright and trademark
  attribution; embedded the full OFL and added derivative copyright notices.
- Added reproducible TTF/WOFF2 builds, outline recipes and SVG proofs, tests,
  complete character atlas, public specimen site, and automated release CI.
- Preview status: automated tests and visual inspection completed. Independent
  native Mongolian/type-designer review has not yet been completed.
- Traditional vertical Mongolian is not included.
