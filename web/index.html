The Mongolian alphabet

All 35 letters, in both cases. Өө and Үү have their own outlines and Unicode mappings. Upright handwritten forms keep the Cyrillic letters distinct.

Every character, accounted for

The original Korean, Latin, and symbol repertoire is preserved. This atlas renders every one of the font’s 11,863 Unicode mappings. Each character is labeled; new additions have a pale blue background.

Made from the same brush

Shared letter shapes use original outlines. New forms follow the source’s tapered strokes, loose rhythm, and open counters. The straight stem of Үү and the descending tail of Уу stay distinct. This first release is a preview; native Mongolian readers and type designers are invited to review it.
