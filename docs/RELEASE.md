Talyn Brush 0.200 redraws the Cyrillic extension around the original Nanum brushwork. The first preview's rigid lowercase is replaced with flowing handwritten forms.

- Rebuilt и, п, т and д around cursive skeletons at the original stroke weight; reshaped к, л, м, ш, б, в, ф and the remaining constructed forms.
- Replaced synthetic pressure ribbons with actual source brush contours, including the marks, crossbars and descenders.
- Refined Өө / Үү, bowl proportions, joins, spacing and capital forms. Fixed winding cancellation in overlapping reflected strokes; added contour/counter regression checks.
- Added a CI-rendered word and small-size proof beside the complete 11,863-character atlas.
- Preserved every original glyph outline, metric, hint program and GSUB rule. License, naming and attribution remain intact.
