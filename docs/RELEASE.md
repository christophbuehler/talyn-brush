Talyn Brush 0.300 combines the revised handwritten Mongolian Cyrillic with German character support.

- Adds Ä, Ö, Ü, ä, ö, ü, ß and ẞ: eight characters missing from the original font.
- Umlauts retain the original Latin bodies and advances, with optically positioned brush dots. Precomposed and decomposed forms shape identically in tests.
- Sharp S uses a long-s head and an open lower s movement. The capital is broader and adjusted to the source's irregular cap height.
- Includes the Cyrillic redraw from 0.200: handwritten lowercase, original brush contours, refined joins and open counters.
- Adds a German word/size proof and a Deutsch sample in the live tester. CI renders all 11,871 Unicode mappings.
- Every original glyph outline, metric, hint program and GSUB rule is preserved. OFL notices now credit both Cyrillic and German extensions.

This remains a **preview**, with visual inspection and automated checks. Independent native Mongolian/type-designer review is welcome. Traditional vertical Mongolian and full Latin Extended coverage are outside this release's scope. No endorsement by the original font authors is implied.
